That's 132 days earlier than the median science fiction campaign on Trailer Math (144 days, across 36 released films), outside the middle half of science fiction rollouts (113 to 194 days out).

## What it's selling

On July 8, alongside the main trailer, the campaign released seven spots, and their titles split two ways: *Fear nothing* and *Risk everything* on one side, *What can be forgiven?* and *Human like everyone else* on the other. The split reads as deliberate, a war film for one audience and a character drama for another, with the action cut aimed at younger men and the emotional one at women. Casting does the rest: Chalamet and Zendaya give the film two of the biggest young stars working. One film, sold several ways.

## How it's doing

*Dune: Part Three*'s first teaser landed 276 days before opening. *Part Two*'s came 185 days before the November 2023 date it was originally cut for, before the strike pushed it to March. The earlier start makes sense, and it's partly because of what we've seen of the film, but also how we'll see it. Shot partly on IMAX cameras, the film reportedly secured an [exclusive domestic IMAX window](https://www.aol.com/articles/dune-part-three-play-imax-211240313.html) for at least its December 18 opening weekend, the same day as *Avengers: Doomsday*. Its IMAX 70mm tickets went on sale in April, about eight months out, following *The Odyssey*, whose IMAX 70mm opening weekend [sold out a year ahead](https://www.fox5ny.com/news/imax-showings-christopher-nolans-odyssey-sell-out-year-before-release-why-is-this). Holding the date against Marvel signals the studio's confidence, and ["Dunesday"](https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/movies/avengers-and-dune-3-are-already-setting-the-foundation-for-a-historic-box-office-dunesday/) is free promotion for both films.

## What to watch

- *Avengers: Doomsday* will probably win the box office, but *Dune: Part Three* should win with critics: I'm calling a Rotten Tomatoes score at least 10 points higher on opening weekend.
- Watch the IMAX 70mm shows. If Dune's opening-weekend 70mm screenings sell out like *The Odyssey*'s did, the eight-month presale paid off.

I'll admit I'm rooting for Dune as the little guy here, even if "little guy" means one blockbuster against another.
